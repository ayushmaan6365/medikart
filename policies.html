COMPLIANCE & LEGAL

Institutional Procurement Policies

Transparent guidelines governing hospital invoicing, calibration warranties, and bio-medical equipment returns.

1. GST Invoicing & Input Tax Credit (ITC) Policy

All transactions executed on MediKart are accompanied by an official Tax Invoice conforming to Section 31 of the CGST Act, 2017. All electro-medical devices and clinical equipment are classified under Harmonized System of Nomenclature (HSN) 9018 attracting 18% GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST for intrastate, or 18% IGST for interstate dispatches).

Invoices are filed through the GST portal (GSTR-1) within the statutory timeline enabling registered medical entities to view and reconcile claims in their GSTR-2B dashboard.

2. Return & Biomedical Replacement Policy

Due to clinical sterility and infection-control regulations, sterile consumables (e.g. gloves, surgical drapes, IV cannulas) cannot be accepted for return once the vacuum blister seal is compromised.

For capital medical devices (ICU monitors, ECG machines, surgical lights, defibrillators), if a factory defect or calibration discrepancy is reported within 10 days of physical delivery, MediKart will dispatch a bio-medical replacement unit immediately without waiting for return shipment inspection.

3. OEM Warranty & Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

All devices are backed by 2 to 3 years of comprehensive manufacturer warranty covering electronic motherboards, sensor transducers, power supplies, and motorized actuators. Hospital facilities can opt for extended 5-year comprehensive AMCs during checkout or quotation negotiation.

4. Privacy & Facility Data Safeguards

Hospital procurement records, GST numbers, and doctor contact parameters are securely stored with 256-bit AES encryption and are never sold or distributed to third-party commercial brokers.
