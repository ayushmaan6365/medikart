Frequently Asked Hospital Questions

Guidance on clinical equipment logistics, GST tax documentation, and onsite bio-medical commissioning.

How do we claim 18% Input Tax Credit (ITC) with our hospital GSTIN?

During checkout, provide your 15-digit state GSTIN in Step 3. MediKart generates an official B2B Tax Invoice under HSN Code 9018 containing your hospital's legal entity name, GSTIN, and separate CGST (9%) / SGST (9%) line entries. This allows 100% tax offset on your hospital's monthly GSTR-3B filings.

What is the standard delivery timeline for heavy medical equipment?

All catalogued medical equipment (ICU patient monitors, ECG machines, electrosurgical units, hospital beds) is shipped via insured temperature-controlled shock-damped medical logistics (Bluedart MedExpress / Safechem). Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days to Tier 1 & Tier 2 cities across India.

Are calibration certificates and OEM warranty provided with the devices?

Yes. Every clinical instrument undergoes pre-dispatch sensor accuracy testing in our NABL-accredited biomedical depot. Each unit arrives with an individual serialized Calibration Certificate, CE declaration of conformity, and 2 to 3 years of comprehensive OEM warranty.

Do you provide onsite installation and nursing staff training?

For all high-acuity capital equipment (ICU monitors, surgical OT lights, electric ICU beds, ultrasound scanners), a certified bio-medical field engineer visits your hospital within 48 hours of delivery to unbox, assemble, calibrate, and conduct hands-on staff demonstration.

How do bulk volume pricing slabs work?

Our product pages feature automated quantity tiers (e.g. 5+ units: 5% extra discount, 15+ units: 12% extra discount). As you increase quantities in the procurement cart, discounts are applied automatically. For large tender inquiries (50+ units), please use our Bulk Quote (RFQ) Form.
